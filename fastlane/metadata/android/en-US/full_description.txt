SpeedRead shows text one word at a time at a fixed point on the screen (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation), so your eyes stop travelling across the page and you can read comfortably at 300, 500 or more words per minute.

<b>Your books</b>
• Import EPUB books and text-based PDFs from your phone, or use "Open with" and "Share".
• A library with covers, progress, search, Reading / Not started / Finished filters and collections.
• Never lose your place: progress is saved as you read.

<b>The reader</b>
• Tap the middle to pause, left or right to jump a sentence; a slider sets the speed from 100 to 1,000 wpm.
• Smart pacing pauses a little longer at punctuation and long words; a gentle start eases you in.
• See the surrounding sentence while paused, or switch to page view to skim and tap a word to continue from there.
• Chunk mode shows short words together; optional speed training raises your speed as you read.
• Bookmarks, highlights and notes, shareable as text.
• Keyboards, Bluetooth page turners, headset buttons and (optionally) volume keys.

<b>The Newsstand</b>
• Top stories from about 70 outlets with free feeds: Irish first, then European and world news. Add your own feeds too.
• Previews, the same story grouped across outlets, topics, search, a reading list and offline reading.
• "Your briefing": each region's biggest stories read back to back, with an optional morning notification.
• Optional breaking-news alerts, with quiet hours and a daily limit.
• Share any web link or text to SpeedRead to speed-read it.

<b>Private by design</b>
No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your books stay on your device. The internet is used only for the news you choose to load. Back up everything to a single file whenever you like.
