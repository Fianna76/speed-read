# StudyPack privacy policy

_Last updated: November 2026_

StudyPack is built to work without collecting anything about you.

## What StudyPack collects

Nothing. StudyPack has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It
does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

## What stays on your device

The question packs and notes PDFs you import, your answer history, quiz results and
settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app
deletes them.

Files are only read when you pick them with the system file picker or send them to
StudyPack with "Open with" or "Share". The app asks for no storage permission.

## Backups

Android's cloud backup only includes StudyPack's settings. A device-to-device transfer (for
example when setting up a new phone) moves everything, as with any app.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's repository.
