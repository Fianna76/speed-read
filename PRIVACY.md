# SpeedRead privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

SpeedRead is built to work without collecting anything about you.

## What SpeedRead collects

Nothing. SpeedRead has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting
service. It does not send your reading activity, your books or your settings anywhere.

## What stays on your device

Your books and PDFs, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, collections,
reading statistics, saved news articles and settings are stored in the app's private
storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them. A backup is only made when you
choose "Back up" in Settings, and it is saved to a location you pick.

## When SpeedRead uses the internet

Only for news, and only from the outlets you have switched on (or feeds you added):

- to load headline feeds and the articles you open, save or (if you turned it on) that are
  downloaded in the background;
- to load news images (which you can limit to Wi-Fi or turn off);
- to fetch a web page you share to SpeedRead.

These requests go directly from your device to the outlet's website, which receives your IP
address and a standard browser-style user agent naming SpeedRead, as with any web visit. Each
outlet's own privacy policy applies to those requests. Pages are opened online in your own
browser.

## Notifications

Breaking-news alerts and the morning briefing are off unless you switch them on. They are
generated on your device from the feeds you follow.

## Permissions

- **Internet** and **network state**: for the news features above, and to keep image and
  background downloads to Wi-Fi when you ask.
- **Notifications** (Android 13+): only requested when you turn on alerts or the briefing.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
